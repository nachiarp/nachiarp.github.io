Vedic Maths

Would you like your child to excel in IMO and competitive exams?

About the Course

Vedic Math is a unique system based on simple rules and principles called sutras, which enable mathematical problems to be solved easily and efficiently — with fast verification of results.

Discovered by the Shankaracharya of Puri in the Vedas as Ganitha Sutras, it presents mathematics as a unified body of knowledge and reduces the burden on students.
